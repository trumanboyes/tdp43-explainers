## One-line note
Benchmark of whether AI genome models can learn to predict TDP-43 cryptic exons, which standard gene annotations leave out. The authors fine-tuned a specialized splicing model (OpenSpliceAI) and a large genomic foundation model (AlphaGenome) on experimentally validated human TDP-43 cryptic exons (267 genes split 80/10/10 by gene: 215 train, 26 validation, 26 test; 411 acceptor and 406 donor sites). HDGFL2, UNC13A, ACTL6B and STMN2 were held out entirely. They then tested on 172/168 mouse cryptic acceptor/donor sites. On the mouse set, fine-tuned OpenSpliceAI raised mean average precision from 0.019 to 0.186 (3' sites) and from 0.022 to 0.174 (5' sites). AlphaGenome with a new head on its frozen backbone scored 0.0. Full-model fine-tuning lifted AlphaGenome to 0.120 and 0.147, about 65% and 84% of fine-tuned OpenSpliceAI. Absolute precision stays modest. Both fine-tuned models showed allele-dependent splicing changes at the ALS/FTD risk locus UNC13A; only full fine-tuned AlphaGenome predicted the exon-allele-linked increase. At HDGFL2, fine-tuning cut background false positives. A genome-wide scan with fine-tuned OpenSpliceAI, cross-referenced with genes enriched in hard-to-culture cell types, flagged new cryptic splicing events in CLDN11 and ARHGAP23. RT-PCR (Sanger-confirmed) detected these events in middle frontal gyrus from a subset of FTLD-TDP cases. They were absent in controls and in cerebellum, and detection tracked semiquantitative oligodendroglial TDP-43 pathology. Known cryptic exons in HDGFL2, UNC13A and STMN2 were positive controls. The CLDN11 event (alternative 3' splice site in the final intron) is predicted to replace the C-terminus with a novel sequence. CLDN11 is highly specific to mature oligodendrocytes and myelin, so this novel sequence is a candidate neoepitope for an oligodendrocyte-specific fluid biomarker of TDP-43 pathology. Limitations: preprint; brain validation was in FTLD-TDP, not ALS, with a small case series that the text does not number, and only some cases were positive; RT-PCR only, with no protein detection; the neoepitope is predicted, with structure modeling only; no CSF/blood data; the benchmark covered only a matched 16 kb local window and did not test AlphaGenome's 1 Mb context. Summary based on the full text (bioRxiv HTML; supplementary figures described in the text, not inspected as images).
