# Paper

**TBK1 eliminates aggregation-prone monomeric TDP-43 through an IFNβ-immunoproteasome pathway in ALS models**
Shohei Sakai, Kotaro Oiwa, Yohei Iguchi, Izzet Mehmet Akcay, Seiji Watanabe, Okiru Komine, Noriko Nagata, Mai Horiuchi, Jemeen Sreedharan, Masahisa Katsuno, Koji Yamanaka (corresponding) et al. (Nagoya University; King's College London; collaborators)
*EMBO Reports*, published Oct 5, 2026 (research article, open access).

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s44319-026-00953-8
- Full text: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1038/s44319-026-00953-8

## One-line note
Aggregation-prone monomeric TDP-43 activates TBK1, which induces IFNβ and the immunoproteasome to clear monomeric TDP-43 in neurons (including human iPSC lower motor neurons) independently of autophagy; IFNAR1 is downregulated in ALS spinal motor neurons with TDP-43 pathology, and heterozygous Tbk1 deletion in SOD1-G93A mice impairs immunoproteasome induction and increases polyubiquitinated protein accumulation at onset. Mechanistic/cell/mouse work; IFNβ not directly measured in mouse tissue; type I IFN signaling in ALS is context-dependent — not a green light to take IFNβ.
