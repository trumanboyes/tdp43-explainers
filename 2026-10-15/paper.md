## One-line note
In cell models, adding NUP50 (a nuclear-pore "gate" protein genetically linked to ALS risk) reduced insoluble, clumped, and hyperphosphorylated TDP-43, but only when it was attached to the nuclear pore; a 41-amino-acid NUP50 fragment was enough to reduce TDP-43 aggregation and neurotoxicity in primary neurons, an ALS-linked NUP50 variant worked less well, and NUP50 partly co-localized with TDP-43 inclusions in ALS/FTD brain tissue. Based on the abstract; cell and neuron cultures plus postmortem tissue only, no in vivo treatment data.
