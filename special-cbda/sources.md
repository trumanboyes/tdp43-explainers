# Sources (key citations checked Oct 24, 2026)

1. **CBDA in TDP-43 ALS mice.** "Preclinical evaluation of cannabidiolic acid as a neuroprotective agent in TDP-43 transgenic mice, an experimental model of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis." *Biomedicine & Pharmacotherapy*, 2025 (published Jun 26, 2025). doi:10.1016/j.biopha.2025.118288 · PMID 40580876. Single study, not replicated.
2. **Human absorption (n=15).** Elder HJ, … Vandrey R, Spindle TR. *Cannabis and Cannabinoid Research* 2025;10(2):e299–e313. doi:10.1089/can.2024.0187. CBDA Cmax 19–25-fold higher than CBD. Two co-authors are from Cultivate Biologics, which makes the product.
3. **Brain penetration in mice.** Anderson LL et al. *J Nat Prod* 2019;82:3047. doi:10.1021/acs.jnatprod.9b00600.
4. **Nausea (animal).** Bolognini D et al. *Br J Pharmacol* 2013;168:1456–70 (PMID 23121618); Rock EM & Parker LA review (PMC5576606); 2021 review noting no clinical trials (PMC8380783).
5. **CANALS (nabiximols/Sativex).** Riva N et al. *Lancet Neurol* 2019;18:155–164. doi:10.1016/S1474-4422(18)30406-X · NCT01776970. 59 people with ALS/PLS, 6 weeks; MAS adjusted difference −0.32 (95% CI −0.57 to −0.07), p=0.013.
6. **Drug interactions.** Cannabinoid–drug interaction review, PMC9871609; riluzole and Epidiolex prescribing information (background).
7. **Hemp law.** Federal: Nov 2025 continuing resolution hemp redefinition (CRS IF13136), 0.4 mg total THC per container, effective date delayed to Dec 11, 2026 (Vicente LLP). New Jersey: P.L.2025, c.215; nj.gov/cannabis/resources/faqs/intoxicating-hemp/.
