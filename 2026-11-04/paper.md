## One-line note
Population-based registry study. From the Piemonte and Valle d'Aosta ALS register (PARALS, 2000–2022; 2,828 incident patients), 1,686 with ≥3 ALSFRS-R assessments in the first 30 months from onset were modeled two ways: a Gaussian mixture model on per-patient linear fits (rate axis: GMM-Slow 31%, −0.44 pts/month, median survival 56.8 mo; Inter-slow 46%, −1.11, 31.6 mo; Inter-fast 17%, −2.23, 18.9 mo; Catastrophic 6%, −3.52, 17.1 mo; C-index 0.755) and a latent-class mixed model with cubic splines (shape axis: SlowLinear 46%, 39.3 mo; PlateauFall 23%, 35.6 mo; LateDecline 15%, 22.7 mo; RapidEarly 16%, 17.6 mo; C-index 0.694). Agreement between partitions was modest (kappa 0.273; 48%). Adding shape to rate barely changed global discrimination (C-index 0.761 vs 0.755; likelihood-ratio test significant) but separated subgroups within rate classes by up to 18 months of median survival (e.g. within GMM-Catastrophic, 30.1 vs 12.0 months). LateDecline was indistinguishable from SlowLinear on total score in year 1 (−0.98 vs −0.83 pts/month), then declined fastest (−1.49 pts/month, months 12–30); it had lower FVC at diagnosis (median 83.5% vs 94.0% SlowLinear and 97.0% PlateauFall), steeper early respiratory subdomain decline, and >3-fold higher odds of losing motor autonomy by 18 months vs SlowLinear. Both models were well calibrated; the combined model had the best accuracy (IPA 39.1%) and net benefit on decision-curve analysis. Of 630 ultrarapid progressors, 58.3% were excluded by the ≥3-visit criterion (median survival 13.1 months), implying trajectory-based studies under-represent the fastest progressors. All four shapes were replicated in the Emilia-Romagna registry (ERRALS, n = 769; LateDecline median 22.0 mo, RapidEarly 13.6 mo). Authors suggest serial FVC monitoring from diagnosis when ALSFRS-R declines only moderately. Limitations (stated by the authors): both cohorts from northern Italy; prospective external recalibration needed before clinical use; ALSFRS-R floor effect and death-related attrition affect late trajectories; cohort truncated at both the fastest and slowest ends; classification requires ≥3 visits. Genotype associations (C9orf72 more frequent in fast classes; SOD1/TARDBP in slow classes) are exploratory. No patients received tofersen. Summary based on the full text (Wiley Online Library).
