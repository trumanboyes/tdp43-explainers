# Paper

**Plasma Lipidome Is Dysregulated in Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis and Associated With Life-Course Adiposity Polygenic Risk**
Zhang L, Zhang L, Zheng Y, Zhuang Z, Tang L, Zheng W, Xia K, Ye S, Yin T, Huang T, Fan D (Department of Neurology, Peking University Third Hospital, Beijing, China, and collaborators)
*Journal of Cachexia, Sarcopenia and Muscle* 2026;17(5):e70410. Published online Oct 21, 2026. Open access.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/jcsm.70410
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42904859/
- Full text (PMC): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13685669/

## One-line note
Single-centre case-control plasma lipidomics study (UPLC-MS/MS, 890 lipid species) in 104 people with sporadic ALS (revised El Escorial definite/probable, within 2 years of onset) and 78 age- and sex-matched healthy controls. After adjusting for age, sex and BMI, 78 lipids differed (FDR < 0.05 and |Cohen's d| > 1.0). Acylcarnitines were the most affected: 30 of 40 detected species were lower in ALS. Several ceramides, phosphatidylcholines and triglycerides were higher, and 22 of 35 lysophosphatidylcholines were lower. A three-acylcarnitine panel (C10:0, C12:0, C18:2) gave a pooled out-of-fold AUC of 0.958 when feature selection was repeated within each training fold (0.977 under standard cross-validation); internal validation only. None of the 78 ALS-associated lipids tracked ALSFRS-R, disease duration, progression rate or onset site after FDR correction. Life-course adiposity polygenic risk scores (birth weight, body fat %, appendicular lean mass, fat-free mass) showed 16 nominal PRS-by-disease interactions (13 acylcarnitines), none surviving FDR, with class-level acylcarnitine enrichment (OR 11.47). No lipid mediated PRS-to-ALS risk. Limitations: one centre; no external cohort or ALS-mimic controls; ALS blood drawn ~5 AM (~7 h fast) vs controls ~8 AM (~10 h fast); metabolic comorbidities and lipid-lowering drugs not excluded; plasma may not reflect CNS. Summary based on the full text (PMC open access).
