## One-line note
Live imaging of GFP-tagged TDP-43 in axons of 40-day human neurons (H9 embryonic stem cell-derived), compared with Rab5, synaptophysin and APP, shows wild-type TDP-43 granules move actively in both directions in roughly equal proportions, with fast, processive outbound (anterograde) runs whose velocities nearly overlap APP; co-immunoprecipitation and proximity ligation assays show TDP-43 associates with the anterograde adaptor KLC1 (more strongly than with the retrograde dynactin subunit DCTN1), with all three kinesin-1 heavy chains KIF5A/B/C (signal lost on KLC1 knockdown), and separately with the kinesin-3 motor KIF1A, suggesting a redundant multi-motor delivery system that TDP-43 pathology could disrupt on several fronts at once. Healthy-neuron, single-cell-line, in-vitro work only: no ALS patient cells or sporadic-ALS models, TDP-43 overexpressed from a plasmid for imaging, reference cargoes imaged separately (no co-transport shown), effects of disrupting transport not tested, and no treatment tested.
