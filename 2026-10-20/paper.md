# Paper

**Immune effects of faecal microbiota transplantation in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis: a pilot multicentre, randomised, masked, sham-controlled, proof-of concept trial**

## One-line note
Randomised 2:1, masked, sham-controlled pilot in 42 adults with ALS of 18 months or less and FVC of at least 70% (28 FMT, 14 sham); two nasojejunal infusions of screened-donor stool at baseline and month 6, no antibiotic preconditioning. Primary endpoint (at least 20% rise in circulating Tregs among CD4+ T cells at 6 months) was not met: 8/18 (44%) vs 2/10 (20%), OR 3.20, 95% CI 0.59–25.36, p = 0.21; mean Treg increase was 2.36 percentage points higher with FMT (p = 0.04). Secondary: ALSFRS-R slope −0.76 vs −1.07 points/month (difference 0.31, 95% CI 0.11–0.51, p = 0.002), but MRC strength score was lower with FMT at month 6 (0.75 vs 0.89, p = 0.042). 32 adverse events with FMT (12.5% severe) vs 14 with sham (28.6% severe), mostly gastrointestinal or infectious; no major safety concerns. Only 29 participants completed the second procedure and 6-month follow-up. Summary based on the published abstract (Europe PMC/PubMed); the full text was blocked by the publisher's site.
