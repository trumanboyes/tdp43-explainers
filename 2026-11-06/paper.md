## One-line note
Bigenic *C. elegans* study of low neuronal TMEM106B C-terminal fragment (TMEM CT; aa 120–254 core) aggregation crossed with models of tau, Aβ1-42, polyQ40, α-synuclein, or TDP-43. Low TMEM CT alone accumulates fewer aggregates and shows no observable neuronal dysfunction (vs high-expressing prior model). Strong neurotoxic synergy with TDP-43 (Table 2: strong function, strong neuron death, strong protein accumulation): mutually elevated detergent-insoluble TMEM CT and elevated total/phosphorylated TDP-43; worse radial locomotion and more GABAergic neuron loss at day 1 adulthood (n>60, N=3; **** P<0.00001 for function/neuron loss; * P<0.05 for protein blots, N=4). Strong synergy with α-synuclein (strong function; strong mutual protein accumulation: insoluble TMEM CT ↑ and insoluble α-synuclein appears; thrashing ****). Milder/partial synergy with tau (strong function impairment; moderate neuron loss by day 3; no clear mutual impact on tau/p-tau or TMEM CT levels). Moderate protein + strong function synergy with polyQ40; no interaction with Aβ1-42 on neuronal function. Implication: secondary TMEM CT co-pathology may blunt therapies that only target primary TDP-43 or α-synuclein. Caveats: preprint; invertebrate model; not a treatment or human trial.
