## One-line note
Computational analysis of ~600 postmortem motor cortex bulk RNA-seq samples (348 donors) from the Target ALS Postmortem Tissue Core. Samples were converted to Reactome pathway-activity profiles (GSVA) and grouped by consensus clustering; k = 7 subtypes (C1–C7) were selected: C1 inflammatory lipid/membrane remodeling, C2 translation/RNA processing, C3 damage response (redox, BAX apoptosis, DNA repair), C4 iron handling/lipid transport/glial remodeling, C5 transporter/lysosomal/metabolic remodeling, C6 mitochondrial/oxidative metabolism, C7 neuronal/synaptic signaling. Donor-held-out projection recovered assignments with mean accuracy 0.929 and balanced accuracy 0.939 (correct subtype in top two for 98.5%). Mapping to an independent BA4 single-nucleus dataset (Pineda et al.; 49 donors, 192,117 nuclei) showed distinct cellular contexts; cell-type marker adjustment attenuated C4 and C7 programs but C2, C3, C5 and C6 programs retained variation. Subtypes formed a continuous pathway-space landscape: an upper C1/C3 inflammatory/damage family, a lower C4–C7 neuronal/metabolic/remodeling family, and a distinct C2 RNA/translation-stress state. The landscape was not significantly associated with disease duration; ALS/FTD family-history-positive and C9orf72-positive samples were more represented in the lower region and family-history-negative samples in the upper region (family-history association persisted after excluding C9orf72-positive samples); C6 showed SOD1-associated enrichment. Six multi-evidence candidate genes: PRKACB, RPL3, COX4I1, UBQLN1, G3BP2, HK1 (not validated targets). Limitations (stated by the authors and noted here): preprint; postmortem late-stage tissue at one time point (cannot show temporal transitions or causation); cell-type marker scores are indirect; anatomical region and technical covariates were associated with cluster membership (sensitivity analyses suggested they did not fully explain it); no independent-cohort validation yet. First author is a student researcher in MIT PRIMES; no specific funding; authors declare no conflicts. Summary based on the full text (bioRxiv PDF).
