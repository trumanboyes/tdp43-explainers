# Paper

**Human iPSC-derived motor neurons with TDP-43 nuclear loss present early ALS pathological phenotypes**
Tze Khee Chan, Shahriar Niroomand, U-Ming Lim, Emelyne Teo, Nikhil Kumar Tulsian, Tayfun Tumkaya, Craig A. Parish, Vladimir Simov, Jia Ling Tiffany Yeo, Jing Wesley Leong, Bhavya Voleti, Michael J. Marino, Rebecca M. Klein, Delphine Fagegaltier, Aaron Zefrin Fernandis (MSD Singapore / Merck & Co.; National University of Singapore collaborators)
*Communications Biology*, published Oct 10, 2026 (open access)

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-026-10832-6
- Article: https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-026-10832-6

## One-line note
Human iPSC motor neurons with TDP-43 locked out of the nucleus show early ALS-like deficits (poor neurite growth, weak axon regen, hyperexcitability, STMN2/KCNQ2 mis-splicing); an STMN2 splice-switching ASO restored splicing and function in vitro.
