## One-line note
New antibodies (LP087/LP088) and immunoassays detect HDGFL2-CE, a cryptic-exon protein made when TDP-43 fails, much more sensitively (LOD ~0.041 pg/µL) and specifically than before; it accumulates in neurons with TDP-43 pathology, and in frontal cortex from 450 people it was higher in FTLD-TDP and FTLD-TDP/MND (vs. controls) and tracked with more pTDP-43, earlier onset and shorter survival; MND-only was not significant after adjustment. Mostly postmortem brain (largely dementia cases); CSF tested only by spike-in, not yet in patient biofluids; group overlap means not a standalone diagnostic; associations, not causation. Full text was blocked to our reader, so this summary relies on the abstract, highlights, full-text excerpts and the university release.
