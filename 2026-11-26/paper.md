**ABBV-CLS-7262 in Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis: A Randomized Clinical Trial**

## What the study did
- ABBV-CLS-7262 is a brain-penetrant activator of eIF2B that dampens the integrated stress response (ISR), a cellular alarm pathway linked to stress granules and TDP-43 aggregation in ALS models.
- Tested as regimen F of the HEALEY ALS Platform Trial: double-blind, placebo-controlled, 74 US centers, March 23, 2023 – October 3, 2024.
- Eligibility: ALS (revised El Escorial possible/probable/definite), ≤36 months from symptom onset, slow vital capacity ≥50%; stable riluzole/edaravone/PB-TURSO allowed.
- Randomized 2:1:1 to 120 mg (n = 155, primary "anchor" dose), 240 mg (n = 79, exploratory) once daily, or regimen placebo (n = 76) for 24 weeks; 50 concurrently randomized placebo participants from another regimen were shared, for 126 placebo in total.
- Primary outcome: Bayesian shared-parameter model of ALSFRS-R and survival (disease rate ratio, DRR; <1 = slowing). Secondary: ALSFRS-R, slow vital capacity, handheld dynamometry, serum NfL, ALSAQ-40; CSF FGF21 as an ISR biomarker.

## Key results
- Primary: DRR 1.06 (95% credible interval 0.85–1.32) for 120 mg vs shared placebo — no slowing. ALSFRS-R slopes −1.00 vs −0.95 points/month. Joint rank analysis P = .94.
- No significant differences in ALSFRS-R, slow vital capacity, muscle strength or ALSAQ-40 at 24 weeks.
- Serum NfL rose about 9.2 percentage points more on 120 mg than placebo (nominal P = .03); because the primary end point failed, the authors call this hypothesis-generating.
- Target engagement: CSF FGF21 fell at week 24 in both dose groups (mean change −6.8 pg/mL at 120 mg, −8.3 pg/mL at 240 mg, vs +0.6 pg/mL on placebo). CSF GDF15 unchanged.
- Safety: adverse events similar to placebo (~89–92% had at least one); fatal adverse events 3.2% (120 mg), 3.8% (240 mg), 2.4% (placebo), none judged drug related; no unexpected safety signals.

## Caveats
- Only 24 weeks of treatment; participants up to 36 months from symptom onset, possibly outside a therapeutic window.
- Responding subgroups cannot be excluded; it is unclear whether ISR activation in ALS is harmful, protective, or both.
- Blood/CSF ISR markers may not reflect activity in motor neurons.
- After this regimen the HEALEY master protocol was revised to enroll people earlier and follow them longer; the drug is being studied for other conditions.
