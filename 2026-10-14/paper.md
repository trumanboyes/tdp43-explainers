## One-line note
Tagging TDP-43 (and FUS) with stronger "anti-aggregation" nuclear-import signals (M9M or IBB) kept the proteins soluble and nuclear even with an ALS-like acetylation-mimic tag defect, lowered the cell's own clump-prone protein through autoregulation, was not toxic to neurons, and reduced STMN2 and HDGFL2 cryptic exons in fibroblasts from two TDP-43 mutation carriers (I383V, G287S). Cell-culture only; no animal or sporadic-patient data yet.
