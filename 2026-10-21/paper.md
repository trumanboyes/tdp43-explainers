## One-line note
Human iPSC-derived spinal motor neurons (4 healthy lines; 4 VCP-mutant ALS lines, R155C/R191Q; 2 TARDBP G298S lines). Two ways of boosting the OGT/O-GlcNAc system were tested: a splice-switching ASO (75 nM) that reduces the cytoplasmic OGT intron-4-retaining transcript (raising OGT mRNA and protein), and the OGA inhibitor Thiamet G (10 µM, 48 h), which raises global O-GlcNAcylation. In healthy motor neurons the ASO raised the nuclear-to-cytoplasmic ratio of PTBP1, U2AF65 and hnRNPK (not TDP-43, FUS, SFPQ or U2AF35), and Thiamet G raised it for U2AF65, hnRNPK, TDP-43, FUS and SFPQ. In VCP-mutant ALS motor neurons neither treatment relocalized TDP-43, FUS, SFPQ, PTBP1 or hnRNPK (the ASO still helped U2AF65; Thiamet G lowered U2AF65/U2AF35 ratios), and in TARDBP-mutant lines neither had an effect. Cell-culture work only, with few patient lines and no sporadic-ALS lines; correlational, not yet tied to specific O-GlcNAc sites. Summary based on the full text (Europe PMC open access).
