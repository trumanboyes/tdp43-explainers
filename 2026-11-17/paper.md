**A Male-Specific Immunometabolic Peripheral Blood Mononuclear Cell Transcriptomic Signature in Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis**

## What the study did
- 30 people with sporadic ALS (15 men, 15 women; revised El Escorial definite/probable/laboratory-supported probable) and 30 sex-matched healthy controls (spouses of participants), recruited at Hanyang University Hospital, Seoul, September 2022–July 2024. People with a family history or a pathogenic variant in ALS genes on whole-exome sequencing were excluded.
- Peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) were isolated and profiled by bulk 3' mRNA sequencing (DESeq2), with a formal sex-by-disease interaction model. Selected genes were checked by qPCR on the same samples. Plasma neurofilament light (NfL) was measured by Simoa.
- Age at sampling, onset age, ALSFRS-R, progression rate (ΔFS), bulbar onset and survival did not differ significantly between men and women with ALS; disease duration at sampling was longer in women (24.33 vs 13.13 months, not significant).

## Key results (verified against the full text)
- Men with ALS vs healthy men: 3,674 differentially expressed genes (1,845 up, 1,829 down; padj <0.1, |log2FC| >1). Women with ALS vs healthy women: only 8.
- Sex-by-disease interaction across all 60 participants: 2,562 significant transcripts (1,253 more up, 1,309 more down in male ALS).
- In men with ALS: mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation / electron-transport genes down (e.g. NDUFA1, UQCRFS1, UQCRQ, SDHC, COX6C); extracellular-matrix, collagen and MMP genes up (e.g. FN1, COL1A1, COL3A1, BGN, MMP2). Confirmed by qPCR; no significant changes in women.
- CD38 (qPCR) was higher in both men and women with ALS than in controls.
- Rapid vs slow progressors: only 6 genes differed after correction (mostly pseudogenes). Plasma NfL correlated with ALSFRS-R, ΔFS, duration and survival, but not with mitochondrial genes or CD38 (r <0.5, p >0.05), and NfL was similar in men and women.

## Caveats (from the authors)
- Small (n=60), single Korean centre; cross-sectional, symptomatic people only, so cause cannot be separated from consequence.
- No other-neurodegenerative-disease control group; subgroup and interaction analyses underpowered; bulk RNA-seq cannot say which cell types change.
- Women had longer disease duration at sampling (not significant), which may have influenced comparisons.
