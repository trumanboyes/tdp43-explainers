## One-line note
Proteomics in TDP-43 yeast (pbp1 deletion) and TAR4/4 TDP-43 mice (Atxn2 ASO, cortical layer V) show ataxin-2 lowering doesn't simply reverse TDP-43 damage but switches on stress-adaptive metabolism: in mouse primary neurons, glycolysis plus IDH1-driven reductive glutamine carboxylation restore ATP without fixing mitochondria (~41–50% rescue; IDH1 knockdown abolishes it, IDH1 overexpression alone protects), and in astrocytes HMGCS1 cholesterol synthesis plus ABCA1 efflux supports neurons; full survival rescue needed neuron–astrocyte co-cultures, protection extended partly to TDP-43 loss-of-function, and wild-type mice showed few changes. Yeast/mouse/mouse-cell work only; TDP-43 overexpression model; metabolic pathways not tested causally in vivo; >90% ataxin-2 knockdown used (the clinical ATXN2 ASO trial, NCT04494256, achieved only modest CSF lowering and was discontinued). The publisher's full-text page was blocked to our reader, so this summary relies on the published abstract, highlights and full-text excerpts plus the full text of the authors' bioRxiv preprint (Jul 28, 2026 version); exact numbers come from the preprint and may differ slightly in the final version.
